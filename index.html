What's This Place?

The Nicholas Conservatory & Gardens is a public botanical facility that stores a huge variety of plants indoors and outdoors. The purpose of this place is for people to have a connection with plants through being educated and have a better understanding of life. It's also a good place to be to feel relaxed from stress.

Location: It's found here in Rockford, Illinois along the banks of Rock River in Sinnissippi Gardens.

The Main Attractions

  • The Tropical Exhibition Area (the main indoor feature of the building)
  • The Rooftop Garden (the second floor feature of the building)
  • The Gift Shop (near the entrance to the Tropical Exhibition Area)
  • The Eclipse Lagoon (one of the main outdoor features)
  • The Sinnissippi Rose Garden (one of the main outdoor features)
  • Seasonal Events

Info

Here's the following information about the Nicholas Conservatory & Garden.

Attractions

  • Explore the Tropical Exhibition Area indoors to see many variety of tropical plants and some animals.
  • During the Tropical Exhibition Area, head upstairs for the Rooftop Garden to view more plants.
  • Go shopping at the Gift Shop.
  • Go outside and walk past or explore the Eclipse Lagoon and see more plants and some animals.
  • Beyond the Eclipse Lagoon, walk to the Sinnissippi Rose Garden to view a huge variety of flowers.

Location

The Nicholas Conservatory & Garden is located in Rockford, Illinois along the banks of Rock River in Sinnissippi Gardens.

Address: 1354 N. 2nd Street Rockford, IL 61107

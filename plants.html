Plants

The Nicholas Conservatory & Garden has a wide variety of plants for indoors and outdoors. Here's at least 20 plants below.

1. Mammie Croton

Type: Shrub

Description: A tropical shrub plant recognized from its thick leaves that curls and twists dramatically.

Interesting Fact: The shades of red, yellow, green, and/or orange leaves colors can change drastically depending on how much light they can get.

2. Hardy Bamboo Palm

Type: Palm Tree

Description: A tropical palm plant that's indigenous to Mexico and looks like a bamboo plant with palm leaves on top.

Interesting Fact: Despite looks almost being the same, they're not related to actual bamboo.

3. Rattlesnake Plant

Type: Herbaceous Perennial

Description: A small tropical plant with light and dark green pattern leaves that comes from the tropical Americas.

Interesting Fact: The name comes from the fact that the leaves look like scaly skin of a rattlesnake.

4. Old Man Palm

Type: Palm Tree

Description: A tall palm tree with "white hair textures" within the trunk, it's from Cuba.

Interesting Fact: The name comes from the fact that the white "hair" within the trunk makes it look like an old man beard.

5. Pitcher Plant

Type: Carnivorous Plant

Description: A small plant with a pitcher-shaped tubes filled with fluid.

Interesting Fact: Pitcher plants use nectar to attract insects to get into their tubes to eat them.

6. Papaya

Type: Herbaceous Perennial

Description: A very tall plant with palm-like leaves structured like an umbrella at the top of the stem.

Interesting Fact: Papaya plants aren't considered trees due to lacking actual wood bark.

7. Bird Of Paradise

Type: Evergreen Clumping Perennial

Description: A medium-sized plant with many bright green leaves alongside some bright orange flowers from South Africa.

Interesting Fact: It's named like that because the shape of the flower and petal makes it look like an exotic bird.

8. Puerto Rican Hat Palm

Type: Palm Tree

Description: A tall palm tree with a very thick trunk and large fan-shaped leaves.

Interesting Fact: It can survive severe coastal winds, drought, poor rocky soils, and cool temperatures.

9. Picasso's Paintbrush Croton

Type: Shrub

Description: A shrub with strapped narrow leaves with vibrant colors of red, yellow, orange, and green leaves.

Interesting Fact: It's named like that due to the leaves being shaped like a paintbrush.

10. Chenille Red Hot Cat's Tail

Type: Shrub

Description: A shrub with green leaves and hanging red long fuzzy flower tassels.

Interesting Fact: It's named like that due to the flower tassels looking like a cat's tail.

11. Dwarf Sanchezia

Type: Shrub

Description: A shrub with very bright colors of green and lime yellow leaves with stripes.

Interesting Fact: The genus name Sanchezia honors Jose Sanchez, a professor of botany from Spain.

12. Texas Star Begonia

Type: Rhizomatous Perennial

Description: A plant with thin brown stems and dark green leaves.

Interesting Fact: Named after the shape of the leaves being star shaped.

13. Horsetail

Type: Non-flowering Perennial

Description: A plant with thin tall green stems with white stripes.

Interesting Fact: It's one of the oldest living plant organisms with ancestors dating back to 350 million years ago.

14. Cacao Tree

Type: Tropical Tree

Description: A tree with long leaves alongside small trunks and hanging cocoa beans.

Interesting Fact: The cocoa beans are used for making chocolate.

15. Silver Brake Fern

Type: Fern

Description: A fern with bright white in the middle and green in the edges of the long leaves.

Interesting Fact: It reproduces with spores rather than seeds or flowers.

16. Carnauba Wax Palm

Type: Palm Tree

Description: A palm tree native to Brazil, has spiraling leaf bases within the trunk.

Interesting Fact: Locals in Brazil name it the "Tree of Life" because every part of the plant has its own usage.

17. Green Apples Bromeliad

Type: Flowering Perennial

Description: A plant with green long leaves and a cup-like shape on top of the plant.

Interesting Fact: Named after the fact that the leaves texture and color look like green apples.

18. Emerald Green Aborvitae

Type: Evergreen Tree

Description: A tall tree with a lot of leaves from almost bottom to top, shaped like a pointy spear.

Interesting Fact: The leaves stay consistantly green during fall and winter.

19. Dwarf Mountain Pine

Type: Woody Shrub

Description: A short shrub with multiple long green needle-shaped leaves.

Interesting Fact: Can thrive in rocky high elevated areas like mountains in the alpines.

20. River Birch

Type: Deciduous Tree

Description: A tall tree with branches and leaves with peeled barks from the thin trunk.

Interesting Fact: Peeled bark trunk outer layers have different colors like white, salmon, peach, etc.
